Privacy Policy

How Crumbiva handles information collected through this website.

Information we collect

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics and does not track visitors. There are no accounts and no online payments.

The enquiry form on the contact page is not currently connected to a mailbox. Anything typed into it is not transmitted or stored. When the form is connected, this page will be updated to describe what is collected and why.

Information you give us directly

When you call or message Crumbiva to place an order, we collect only what is needed to prepare and hand over that order. Typically that is your name, a contact number, the order details and a pickup time. We use it for that purpose only and do not sell or share it.

Third party services

The typefaces used on this website are loaded from Google Fonts. That means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you load a page, and Google receives your IP address as part of serving the font files. No other third party services are used.

The website is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard web server request logs for security and reliability.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Call 0490 875 811 to make a request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
