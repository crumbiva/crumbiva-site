About Crumbiva

Small kitchen. Big table.

Cookies, curries and everything in between, made by hand in one kitchen in Doncaster East.

Crumbiva is a home kitchen in Doncaster East, Victoria. We bake cookies in small batches and cook North Indian food the way it is cooked at home, for people who would rather have something made for them than something taken off a shelf.

Everything is made to order. Nothing sits in a cabinet waiting for a buyer. When you collect it, it was made for you the same day.

I make everything the way I would make it for my own family. Nothing leaves this kitchen any other way.Kanu

Our founder

Kanu

Kanu has been baking for more than twelve years. It began the way these things usually begin. A kitchen, an occasion, and a house full of people who expected to be fed.

She is a mother of two, and for most of those years the baking happened around school runs, homework and family life. Cookies came first. Then cakes. Then everything else that could come out of a good oven.

The cooking grew alongside the baking. Kanu has catered professionally across India, Singapore, Sydney and Melbourne, and has cooked for more than seventy house parties over a decade. Different cities, different kitchens, different crowds at the table.

What never changed was the standard. Real butter. Good nuts. Jaggery where jaggery belongs. Nothing added that does not need to be there.

Crumbiva is that same table, set a little wider. The cookies are made in small batches. The curries are cooked the day you collect them. Everything is made the way Kanu makes it at home, because it is home.
