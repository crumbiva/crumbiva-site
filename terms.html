How ordering from Crumbiva works.

Ordering

Orders are placed by phone on 0490 875 811. This website does not take orders and does not process payments. Nothing on this site is a checkout.

Pickup

Crumbiva is a pickup only service. Orders are collected from Doncaster East, VIC. The exact pickup address is provided when your order is confirmed.

Availability and timing

Orders are prepared fresh for your scheduled pickup, with same day preparation where applicable. Please contact us in advance so we can confirm availability and timing before you plan around an order. Bulk orders for parties and functions need notice.

Pricing

Prices shown on this website are in Australian dollars and apply to the items as described. Prices and availability may change. The price confirmed on the phone at the time of ordering is the price that applies.

Dietary requirements

Vegan and no onion or garlic options are available on request. Cookie flours and sweeteners can be selected by enquiry. Please tell us about any dietary requirements when ordering so we can advise what is possible.

Recipes

The recipes published on this website are general baking guidance. They are not the recipes used in the Crumbiva kitchen.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be raised by phone on 0490 875 811.
