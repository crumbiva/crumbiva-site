Our Founder

Meet Kanu

12+ Years of Baking. 70+ House Parties. 2 Countries. And Now, Crumbiva.

For over 12 years, Kanu has been baking cookies, cakes and creating bakes for celebrations big and small. Over the past decade, she has catered for more than 70 house parties across Singapore and Australia, bringing together experience, creativity and a love for beautifully made food.

Crumbiva is the next chapter of that journey, bringing handcrafted cookies, thoughtful gifting and custom creations together under one brand.
