Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies Soft and chewy

Ingredients

  • 150 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 120 g brown sugar
  • 60 g caster sugar
  • 1 egg, plus 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 250 g plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 200 g dark chocolate chips

Method

  1. Beat the butter and both sugars until pale and fluffy.
  2. Add the egg, yolk and vanilla. Beat until fully combined.
  3. Fold in the flour, baking soda and salt, then the chocolate chips.
  4. Chill the dough for at least 1 hour. This is what keeps them thick.
  5. Roll into 60 g balls, place on a lined tray, and bake at 180 C for 11 to 13 minutes.
  6. The centres should still look underdone. Leave them on the tray for 10 minutes to set.

Chilling the dough is the single biggest difference between a flat cookie and a thick one. Do not skip it.

Jaggery and Fennel Cookies Gud Saunf

Ingredients

  • 130 g ghee or unsalted butter, softened
  • 140 g jaggery, grated or powdered
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 200 g whole wheat or plain flour
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
  • 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Warm the milk and dissolve the jaggery into it. Let it cool.
  2. Beat the ghee, then add the jaggery milk and beat again.
  3. Fold in the flour, fennel, cardamom, baking powder and salt to form a soft dough.
  4. Rest the dough for 20 minutes.
  5. Shape into small rounds and bake at 170 C for 13 to 15 minutes.
  6. Cool completely. They firm up as they cool.

Jaggery burns faster than sugar, so keep the oven a little lower and watch the last two minutes.

Pistachio Shortbread Cookies Buttery and crunchy

Ingredients

  • 200 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 80 g icing sugar
  • 240 g plain flour
  • 40 g cornflour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 120 g shelled pistachios, roughly chopped
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Beat the butter, icing sugar and vanilla until smooth, not airy.
  2. Fold in the flour, cornflour, salt and three quarters of the pistachios.
  3. Bring the dough together by hand. Do not overwork it.
  4. Roll into a log, wrap, and chill for 2 hours until firm.
  5. Slice into 8 mm rounds and press the remaining pistachios onto the tops.
  6. Bake at 160 C for 14 to 16 minutes until the edges are just barely golden.

Shortbread has no raising agent, so the shape you cut is the shape you get. Chill properly before slicing.

Ragi and Oat Cookies Wholesome

Ingredients

  • 120 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 90 g jaggery or coconut sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 90 g ragi flour
  • 90 g rolled oats
  • 60 g whole wheat flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 40 g chopped almonds or walnuts

Method

  1. Beat the butter and jaggery until combined, then beat in the egg.
  2. Mix the ragi, oats, wheat flour, baking soda and cinnamon in a separate bowl.
  3. Combine the wet and dry, then fold in the nuts.
  4. Rest the dough for 30 minutes so the oats soften.
  5. Scoop onto a lined tray and flatten slightly with the back of a spoon.
  6. Bake at 170 C for 12 to 14 minutes.

These have a nuttier, denser crumb than a plain cookie. That is the ragi, and it is the point.
